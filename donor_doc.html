SEAL Sponsorship Program

Security Alliance (SEAL) is a nonprofit dedicated to protecting the crypto ecosystem through incident response, threat intelligence, security frameworks, and certification standards. Your sponsorship directly funds the people and infrastructure that make this work possible — while giving your team meaningful access to SEAL's intelligence, community, and expertise.

All of SEAL's core work remains free and open. Sponsorship adds private value on top of public goods.

Why SEAL

In 2025, SEAL handled over 1,800 incident tickets and coordinated 125+ war rooms in response to active exploits. Drainer-related losses fell to $84 million — an all-time low — down from nearly $1 billion cumulative over prior years. SEAL's total annual operating cost: $2M.

That works out to roughly $1,100 per incident handled. A single smart contract audit costs $50–200k. The Bybit exploit alone was $1.4 billion — more than 700x SEAL's entire annual budget.

SEAL operates with ~5 full-time staff. The rest — 28 SEAL 911 responders, framework stewards, Intel contributors — are volunteers who donate thousands of hours of senior security expertise because they believe in the mission. Initiative leads are compensated at well below market rate. Your sponsorship funds the coordination layer that makes this volunteer network effective.

Where your money goes: The majority of sponsorship revenue goes directly to people — the analysts, engineers, and coordinators who respond to incidents, produce threat intelligence, and develop security standards.
Current runway: SEAL publishes full financial transparency reports. We operate lean by design — every dollar goes to work immediately, which also means we rely on sustained sponsorship to continue operating. We'll share our current financial position openly with any prospective sponsor.
