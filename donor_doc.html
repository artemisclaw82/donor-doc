SEAL Sponsorship Program

Your sponsorship directly funds the people and infrastructure that protect the crypto ecosystem — while giving your team meaningful access to SEAL's intelligence, community, and expertise.

All of SEAL's core work remains free and open. Sponsorship adds private value on top of public goods.

Why SEAL

In 2025, SEAL handled over 1,800 incident tickets and coordinated 125+ war rooms in response to active exploits. Drainer-related losses fell to $84 million — an all-time low — down from nearly $1 billion cumulative over prior years. SEAL's total annual operating cost: $2M.

That works out to roughly $1,100 per incident. A single smart contract audit costs $50–200k. The Bybit exploit alone was $1.4 billion — more than 700x SEAL's entire annual budget.

Where your money goes: The majority of sponsorship revenue goes directly to people — the analysts, engineers, and coordinators who respond to incidents, produce threat intelligence, and develop security standards. SEAL operates with ~5 full-time staff; the rest are volunteers who donate thousands of hours of senior security expertise because they believe in the mission.
Financial transparency: SEAL publishes full financial transparency reports. We operate lean by design — every dollar goes to work immediately. We'll share our current financial position openly with any prospective sponsor.
